Found your company

Twelve quarters. Five corporations. One winner. Everyone starts with $100,000: $40,000 personal cash, $40,000 corporate cash and $20,000 in business assets.

How to win

Set prices, production and investments before each quarter. Rivals decide independently; all orders resolve together. The highest founder net worth after quarter 12 wins.

Unsold products are written off. Expansion adds capacity next quarter, and research improves customer appeal. Personal cash is separate from corporate funds. This prototype has no network multiplayer or share trading yet.
