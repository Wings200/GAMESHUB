V0.19.0 · SEVEN FUNCTIONAL SECONDARY GADGETS. THROWABLE AIM: Hold MMB or G to show a colored trajectory and endpoint marker; look around while holding to adjust the predicted throw, then RELEASE the key/button to throw. Preview uses each projectile's real flight/bounce/fuse collision simulation (including landing C4 and impact contact). It disappears as soon as you release or pause. Nonthrowable Barbed Wire still places immediately. ATTACK: Choose STRIKER and equip Frag Grenades in either MMB or G. Throw up to two grenades that physically bounce and detonate after a 3.2-second fuse, dealing full damage to players and target dummies. Frag Grenades splinter wooden barricades but cannot damage gypsum walls. SOFT BREACH CHARGES: Striker gets two wall-mounted explosives for intact soft gypsum or wooden barricades within 2.75m. Tap the selected MMB or G slot to plant, wait 0.9 seconds for placement, and tap again to detonate. A large physical opening is created in the gypsum or boards. Alternatively, look directly at an undetonated charge and tap F to recover and reuse it. Reinforced steel blocks placement. Impact Grenades and C4 still break both wooden barricades and unreinforced soft walls. FLASHBANGS: equip two timed flash grenades, with a 1.75-second fuse, visible bounce and a completely opaque pure-white screen (no visibility of the world, weapons or HUD) for 2.2 seconds, followed by a very slow 3.8-second fade, when looking toward an unblocked flash. Look away or stand behind solid cover to avoid blindness; flashes cause NO HP damage. SMOKE GRENADES: Striker can equip two physical 1.8s-fuse canisters in either gadget slot. Each releases a thick 3D expanding smoke cloud (~3.4m radius) that hides targets behind its opaque core, lasts about 16 seconds, and gradually dissipates. Standing inside dense smoke also obscures your view. Smoke doesn't damage HP or block bullets. DEFENSE: Choose SENTRY and equip C4 / Nitro Cell plus Impact Grenades. C4 is thrown with its assigned key and remotely detonated by pressing that key again; approach a landed C4, aim directly at it and tap F to retrieve it for reuse. Impact Grenades explode on physical contact with a tighter 2.0-meter radius. Impacts deal up to 60 HP; C4 and Frag Grenades kill at point-blank range on either side, with distance-based falloff. All have visible 3D models and blast effects against player HP and target dummies. Frag Grenades destroy wooden barricade boards but cannot damage gypsum walls. Impact Grenades and C4 break wooden barricades and unreinforced gypsum tiles; reinforced steel remains protected. Sentry can alternatively equip Barbed Wire for enemy-only contact damage, slowing, and V melee destruction. Five gadget options still await physical implementation.
