THE LAB — MECHANICS BEFORE MAPS

No city. No facility. No map development yet. A flat tactical testing ground for movement, recoil, ADS, bullet damage, speed and original attacker/defender gadgets. This is single-player testing—not a live 5v5 match.

ATTACKER MECHANICS
TEST CONTROLS

WASD: move · Shift: sprint · Ctrl: hold crouch · Z: toggle prone · Q/E: lean · Mouse: look

LMB: shoot/hipfire · Hold RMB: ADS (hipfire crosshair hides; sight reticle appears) · V: cycle Holo A / Holo B / Red Dot / Iron · R: reload · 1/2: rifle/sidearm.

Attacker — G: attach charge to soft panel (within 7m; detonates automatically), X: pulse reveal test targets.

Defender — F: install reinforcement on aimed panel (3-second animation; no movement or firing until complete); G: shield; X: proximity sensor. Move outside then re-enter its radius to trigger it.

Soft-wall penetration: bullets punch individual holes and travel through at reduced damage, but do not destroy the wall. Line up the rear targets behind the two wall panels to test shooting through. Only a breach charge removes an unreinforced panel.

Esc: return to menu to switch sides · F3: coordinates and collision diagnostics.

Choose a side. Then click ENTER TEST RANGE.
