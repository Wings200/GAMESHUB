TEST CONTROLS

WASD: move · Shift: sprint · Hold ALT: quiet, slow walk · Ctrl: crouch · Z: prone · Tap Q/E: hold left/right lean · tap the same side to reset · Mouse: look. Operator speed is fixed.

LMB: shoot/hipfire · Hold RMB: ADS (hipfire crosshair hides; sight reticle appears) · V: cycle Holo A / Holo B / Red Dot / Iron · R: reload · 1/2: rifle/sidearm.

Attackers — Control a wheeled drone for 45 seconds before your operator spawns; use X to scan and Twitch can use G to shock nearby electronics. Four allied AI operators enter with you against five defending bots. Split between Sites A and B, take cover and clear angles. Stand anywhere on legal floor inside EITHER bomb room, hold F while stationary to plant where you stand. Defend it after planting. G uses your selected operator's unique gadget; X is recon/utility. Press 3 after spawning to deploy another scouting drone.

Defenders — You and four friendly AI protect BOTH bomb rooms. During the 45-second preparation phase, you cannot leave the building. After action begins, leaving the building reveals your location to all five attackers (runout detection). Board doors or windows with F, reinforce soft panels, deploy shields with G and proximity sensors with X. Enemy attackers arrive from multiple directions.

Barricade interaction — Aim toward a door or window and hold F to install (defenders) or remove (either side). Shoot individual wooden planks to break them; press Space near a cleared side window to vault through. Barricades block players, bots, and bullets while intact.

Round results — Rounds continue if you die while surviving allies fight; you spectate the squad. Team elimination, successful plant/disable or objective timer decides the winner. Enter starts the next round; Esc opens the menu.

Soft-wall penetration: bullets punch tiny individual holes and travel through at reduced damage, but do not destroy the wall. Line up the rear targets behind the two wall panels to test shooting through. Only a breach charge removes an unreinforced panel.

Esc: return to menu to switch sides · F3: coordinates and collision diagnostics.
