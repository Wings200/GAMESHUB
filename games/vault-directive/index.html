OVERSEER TRAINING

FIELD MANUAL

Start with your command post and generator, with zero residents. Build living quarters, clean water, and food production, then invite survivors with + SURVIVOR. The six-step tutorial teaches the basics. Earn research XP and buy permanent bonuses in SKILLS.

Select rooms in the cross-section to staff, upgrade, seal, or defend them. Select excavatable dirt next to your rooms to construct more rooms. Assign residents by selecting them in the roster, then clicking a friendly room.

Power, food, and water keep your people alive. Shortages erode loyalty. Low loyalty can trigger a rebellion. Captured sectors remain hostile territory and can spread unless sealed off. Rebels with food, water, and medical facilities can endure a siege much longer.

If your command falls in Standard mode, you can resist from exile, negotiate, or found another vault. Hardcore ends on overthrow. Progress saves locally in this browser.
