Compare real car recordings before we replace REDLINE V11.2's working audio. Play them with music off and headphones on. These are unaltered originals, not finished in-game loops; choose based on engine character, not loudness.

This lab does not change the game. No new engine sounds are enabled in races yet. Recordings are played from their source at Wikimedia Commons; if a source cannot load, use its linked original page.

Which sounds belong in REDLINE?

Each role can use a different recording, or you can choose none. The final engine bank will require hand-picked loop sections and multi-RPM transitions before it replaces the current system.
