# REDLINE V12 — Outdoor engine sound audition, round 2

## Why round 1 was rejected

User listening feedback: the initial Maserati, Ferrari 250 GTO and 458
samples sounded **as though recorded inside the cabin**, not outside near
an engine/exhaust. The first listening page did not meet the intended
close, punchy exterior sound character.

## Round 2 (outside recordings, unmodified at 1× speed)

The listening page `games/redline/audio-v12-lab.html` now has four
**actual outdoors** motorsport audio clips, each documented with
the recordist's camera position at the Goodwood Festival of Speed.
They were originally recorded and uploaded by **Edvvc (Ed Pond)**.

1. **American V8s / trophy truck**: [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trophy_Truck_and_American_V8s.ogg), [original audio](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c6/Trophy_Truck_and_American_V8s.ogg). ~82 seconds, trucks idling and other V8s at Goodwood 2009.
2. **Aston Martin V12 Vantage**: [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aston-Martin-V12-Vantage.ogg), [original audio](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f5/Aston-Martin-V12-Vantage.ogg). ~9 seconds, rev limiter and pullaway at Goodwood 2009.
3. **Lamborghini Gallardo LP570-4 Superleggera**: [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Gallardo_LP570-4_Superleggera.ogg), [original audio](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0b/Lamborghini_Gallardo_LP570-4_Superleggera.ogg). ~10 seconds, V10 pullaway at Goodwood 2010.
4. **Nissan GT-R SpecV**: [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nissan_GT-R_SpecV.ogg), [original audio](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/69/Nissan_GT-R_SpecV.ogg). ~8 seconds, GT-R pulling away at Goodwood 2009.

All four source pages list **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
3.0 Unported**. Preserve authorship, links, change notices, and
applicable ShareAlike requirements if any modified audio is distributed.

The four media URLs were verified against the original-file destinations
shown on Wikimedia Commons source pages, although audio playback in
the deployed page has not been manually confirmed.

## Limitations

- Outdoor field recordings may still be too distant, windy, crowded or
  uneven in level for REDLINE's *continuous engine loops*.
- Passing-car Doppler pitch shifts are not clean load/RPM transitions.
  These clips are **audition examples**, not completed engine audio loops.
- The V11.2 race mixer, all account/worker paths and both maps remain
  unchanged. No engine replacement was made in this update.
- If none pass listening, search for dedicated close-mic outdoor
  stationary exhaust recordings or a properly licensed game-ready RPM
  sound pack (idle/low/mid/high under consistent recording conditions)
  rather than distorting or endlessly EQing these field samples.
