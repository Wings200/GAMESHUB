FIELD MANUAL // PHASE ONE

HOW TO BUILD.

Begin at the hatch. Switch to SURFACE, click a scavenging location and wait for your character to return with material. Switch UNDERGROUND and select SHOVEL. You can dig dirt or clear collapsed rubble only when the target has an open neighbor you can reach from your entrance.

Place timber frames on excavated blocks to hold up tunnels. Deeper excavations are less stable, especially without supports. Unlock stronger tunnel types in TUNNEL TECHNOLOGY. To build a room, excavate a complete 2×2 square and reinforce at least two of those four blocks. Choose a room blueprint and click its upper-left block.

Once you construct a room, its footprint is sealed into a structural room module. Dismantling a room clears its footprint; dismantling a support refunds some materials. Resources are spent only after a valid action. This is a separate prototype and does not recruit or simulate residents yet.
