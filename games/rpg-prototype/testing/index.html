GAMESHUB / RPG SYSTEMS LAB / W20.87
SETTLEMENT TESTING / HAVEN JUNCTION

Enter the Haven workshop directly with your saved materials. Build homes, produce food and water, generate power, recruit residents and inspect happiness.

W20.87 · ACTUAL RADIATION HP LOSS & HUD FIX: Radiation now hurts real current HP even when the player was already wounded, in addition to limiting maximum survivable health. Both ordinary HP bars and the power-armor HP dial use the full 100-HP scale, so dropping to 75 HP visibly shows 75% rather than a deceptively full 75/75 bar. The unusable health portion is marked red on both ordinary bars; the separate RAD gauge and counter continue showing accumulated rads. Radiation can cause a real death and trigger the first-person death sequence. Radiation Flush removes radiation and restores the health cap but does not heal missing HP.

W20.86 · HAZARD SCENE REPAIR & COLLISION PASS: Moved the radiation source entirely clear of the raised training ramp. Replaced the billboard and buried miniature drums with a ground-level waste depot: rusted full-size ribbed drums, warning decals, an overturned tank, bent metal fence, irregular leaking pools and debris. Solid debris now blocks bullets and movement. The massive solid-green slope is now a textured weathered-concrete ramp with visible slab joints. Radiation intensity and medical treatment remain unchanged. This is still a testing field, not a finished campaign location.

W20.85 · RADIATION & FULL RPG REBUILD FOUNDATION: Radiation is no longer a fake zeroed-out power-armor gauge. A visible, physically located contaminated drum spill on the east side of the field now applies persistent radiation over time, reduces your real maximum HP, and appears on both the regular HUD and power armor's live dial. An occupied power frame reduces exposure. DOC VALE sells Radiation Flush medical treatment, which removes rads without magically restoring lost HP; use a medkit separately to heal. Radiation survives reloads and respawn, with bounded save migration. The full-game redesign standards, current gaps and phased acceptance tests are now documented in WASTELAND_DESIGN_BIBLE.md.

W20.84 · MODULAR ROOF MODELS & SCALE FIX: Roofs now share the exact 3×3-metre footprint of timber foundations and fit above 2.7-metre walls. The corrugated flat roof has individual scavenged sheet-metal panels, seams and visible framing; a new gabled scrap roof uses two physically slanted panels, a ridge cap and rafters. With grid snap enabled, roofs center on foundation bays or the bay adjoining an actual wall edge, never on top of the wall itself. The preview calls out ROOF BAY SNAPPED. Roofs require a properly aligned supporting wall, and storing or scrapping that wall is blocked until its roof is safely removed.

W20.83 · WALL CORNER JOINING & EDGE SNAP: Walls and doorframes can now meet at proper ninety-degree corners and T-junctions without being rejected as overlapping structures. With grid snap enabled, a wall near an existing wall endpoint or foundation edge automatically aligns into place, and the preview reports CORNER SNAPPED. True collisions with rocks, other props and crossed walls still block construction. The previous duplicate collision check for placed structures is removed, so valid joins survive saving and reloading.

W20.82 · REAL SETTLEMENT OBJECT STORAGE: F no longer gives components back. It puts a complete player-built object into workshop storage and adds a (1), (2) and so on beside that object in the construction menu. Choose its blueprint and place the stored copy without spending materials; the count decreases when placed, even after reloading. X remains the scrap key for both pre-existing settlement objects and your own structures, permanently removing them for salvaged materials. Storing and scrapping never create duplicate supplies, and existing saves migrate with empty storage.

W20.81 · TRUE SETTLEMENT SCRAP & POWER ARMOR GANTRY: X now permanently scraps junk, old settlement fixtures, or any player-built structure for actual recovered materials; F stores your placed structures for a full construction-material refund. The original Haven weapon bench and the individual floor, walls and roof of a derelict shack are editable scrap targets, with every removal saved. The unnecessary salvage sorting table is no longer offered as a new blueprint, but already-placed ones remain recoverable. Power armor service racks now have an open yellow A-frame, overhead hoist, hydraulic arms, bracing and safety markings in the style of a wasteland maintenance gantry.

W20.80 · PLACEABLE WORKBENCHES & WORLD SALVAGE: A new WORKSHOPS category provides a physical weapon-modification bench, a power-armor service rack (bring a real frame nearby) and a salvage sorting table. Built weapon benches can now modify guns with E, the same as Haven's original fixed bench—there is still no U remote shortcut. Haven also has distinctive abandoned junk that can be scrapped permanently in building mode: aim at a piece and press X to turn it into actual wood, steel, copper, circuitry and other parts. Salvaged clutter disappears from the world and stays gone across reloads; each object pays out only once. Player-built structures still store for a full material refund.

W20.79 · LIVING SETTLEMENT WORKSHOP: A dedicated settlement-testing entry and eight build categories replace the single flat list. Press Tab to switch categories, 1–7 to choose pieces, and B to exit. Your gun and gun UI disappear while building and return when you leave. Crops, pumps, purifiers, beds, guard posts, sentry turrets, generators, lamps, storage and a powered recruitment beacon now have distinctive 3D models and real materials costs. The top dashboard tracks residents, food, water, electricity demand, defense, beds and changing happiness; recruiting a settler requires enough powered beacon capacity, food, water and sleeping space.

W20.78 · HAVEN JUNCTION WORKSHOP BUILDING: Enter Haven by the foreman and weapon bench, then press B to build. Choose one of seven original scavenged structures, see a green or red world-space preview, rotate or turn off grid snapping, and place pieces only when you own the wood, steel, cloth and other required components. Buildings block movement and gunfire where solid, persist across reloads, and can be stored by aiming and pressing X (or undoing the last with Z) for a full material refund. Roofs need walls or doorframes nearby. This is the construction foundation; settler jobs, electrical wiring, crops and water production are future systems.

W20.77.2 · CORRECT CORNER PEEK DIRECTION: Guards now choose left or right based on their facing and the actual firing lane. A corner peek holds one shoulder until the peek finishes, preventing the character from leaning the other way halfway through. Automatic cover leaning clears its old direction whenever an explicit corner peek begins.

W20.77.1 · RAISED RIFLES & PHYSICAL CORNER PEEKING: NPC eyes now follow the animated head rather than an invisible chest-height point. When a rock exposes only the player's head, raiders lift their actual rifle and aim from its real muzzle. Around corners they turn and lean their rendered torso, head and held gun, with both hands gripping the rifle; a shoulder peek only counts when the projectile path is truly clear. Existing cover routes, ammunition and reloading still govern attacks.

W20.77 · ENEMY INTELLIGENCE & COMBAT OVERHAUL: Guards no longer fire sideways before facing their target or draw every missed shot far ahead of someone standing still. Steady observation improves accuracy without granting perfect aim; moving targets, recoil, distance and suppression still matter. Scouts, raiders and enforcers now navigate around blocking cover, commit to firing lanes between flanks, retain a short aim memory on brief occlusions, and use smoother speed-based steps, strafing lean, search glances, reloads and hit reactions.

W20.76.1 · FIRST-PERSON DEATH CINEMATIC: On fatal damage your view staggers down to your knees, pauses, then pitches into the ground with a brief impact jolt. Only after the fall fades out does YOU DIED appear. The Haven respawn and recoverable power-armor frame are unchanged.

W20.76 · DEATH & POWER ARMOR RECOVERY: Fatal damage now plays a third-person collapse before showing a death screen. Respawn at Haven with your backpack, progression and world changes intact. A power-armor frame stays where you fell. The map shows only your most recently worn parked frame, and you may track or untrack it without replacing your own waypoint.

W20.75.2 · SETTLEMENT WEAPON WORKBENCH: Weapon modifications require physically walking to Haven's workshop bench and pressing E nearby. The U shortcut and remote-workbench menu button are gone; the firing lane no longer contains a weapon workbench.

W20.75.1 · READ-ONLY DIAGNOSTICS: F3 reveals performance and gameplay status without a keybind cheat sheet. Free healing, enemy resetting/freezing, station teleporting and dummy-armor editing are no longer accessible to players. Quick-jump buttons to the combat arena, trading row and power-armor frame have been removed. Find frames in the world and use ordinary gameplay controls instead. Both compasses remain visible.

W20.75 · FACTION POWER ARMOR & FOUND FRAMES: Power armor only comes from visibly armored combatants, trusted Iron Dominion/Rust Vultures quartermasters, or abandoned powered chassis. Earn 35 faction reputation to trade their T-45 parts and fusion cores, or 65 for advanced T-60 parts; formal membership is not required. Enforcers and raiders may wear armor in combat. Three incomplete, damaged T-45 frames with cores can be discovered and configured in the testing field. Each frame's parts, core and location save independently; no power armor plate crafting.

W20.74.1 · POWER ARMOR REPAIRS: Armor plates cannot be crafted. Recover parts from armored enemies, faction dealers or found frames. Repairs use tier-dependent crafting components; higher-tier modules require rarer and more numerous materials, with costs scaling to their damage.

W20.74 · POWER ARMOR CONFIGURATION: The six-part condition figure now sits at the center of the service screen. Select any body section, inspect matching armor plates from your backpack, preview durability and protection, then deliberately install, remove, or repair that slot.

W20.73 · WEAPON WORKBENCH: Choose an owned weapon, inspect its installed assemblies, select a replacement part, and preview performance and exact material requirements before crafting or installing. Nothing is spent just by inspecting an option.

W20.72.1 · BARTER CONFIRMATION: Adding an item to either side now stages it for a single combined trade. Review everything you are buying and selling, check the net cap balance, and press Confirm Trade once. Closing or clearing the offer exchanges nothing.

W20.72 · VENDOR TRADING: Choose exact buy and sell quantities, review live cap totals, sell reserve ammunition, and see your wallet and backpack space beside merchant stock. Sold-out goods, unaffordable offers and equipped gear are clearly marked. Existing merchant prices and saved stock remain intact.

W20.71 · LOOT TRANSFERS: Looting now shows container contents beside your backpack, lets you choose stack quantities, reports carry capacity, and clears a case's glow when it is empty. Enemy drops and Take All use the same inventory transfer rules.

W20.70 · LOOT CASE GLOW: Loot cases no longer have giant floating LOOT labels. Their physical lids, seams, and latches glow and pulse with a rarity-colored halo. Walk close and press E to search as before.

W20.69.3 · ALWAYS-ON COMPASS: The field compass and power-armor compass remain visible even when no destinations or targets are present. Only combat enemies, active quests, discovered locations, and custom waypoints can create navigation markers; irrelevant NPCs and test landmarks stay off the HUD.

W20.69 · QUEST NAVIGATION: Tracked objectives are synchronized with the map and both navigation HUDs. Completed quests are kept in inactive history; empty or inactive objectives no longer leave stray markers.

W20.68 · CHARACTER TERMINAL: Status, backpack pages, quest tracking, and the explorable field map are available from the in-game interface.

W20.67 · NAVIGATION: Both HUDs now show explored locations and map-set custom waypoints with direction and distance. The weapon workbench uses only the left-hand owned-gun list; its redundant dropdown is gone. Existing merchant stock and equipment systems remain unchanged.

W20.62 · DEVELOPER ITEM GRANTS: Admins can open the Developer Panel to search implemented items, grant weapons, armor, ammo and materials, or add caps. Equip granted firearms through I / Backpack. Free crates, resource caches, and cap handouts have been retired from the test field. Normal loot, vendors and quests remain. Visit Station 7 to craft weapon mods with materials you own.

W20.62 · PLAYER INVENTORY Equipment and caps you already owned stay saved. New test saves start with no free guns, ammo reserves or field caches. Developer item grants are limited to signed-in admins; buyers still use actual vendor prices. The map, NPCs, power armor, weapon workbench and quest systems remain available.

W20.10 · REAR ENTRY & SUIT SILHOUETTE: Power armor has deploying rear stairs, an opening central spine, two-joint hip/knee IK, real handle-gripping arms and separate boot contacts. The camera stays outside until the hatch shuts. NPCs must face you before firing; tracers originate at their visible muzzle.

W20.18 · FAIR ENEMY RADAR: Red dots only reveal enemies after they have spotted you or you have engaged them. Contacts fade shortly after enemies lose sight; undiscovered patrols remain hidden. In power armor, tap E to search nearby enemy drops or crates, or hold E to exit.

W20.16 · COMBAT & POWER FRAME: Compact armor silhouette and clearance, deeper rear boarding, softer boot impacts, fusion cores lasting 25% longer, close-quarters enemy tracking, single-render-layer NPCs, concealed enemy tactics, and a bottom red-dot hostile compass.

W20.15 · COCKPIT CLEANUP: Removed the two obsolete teal camera-mounted LED strips that followed the player while wearing power armor. The amber gauges and armor-condition figure stay unchanged.

W20.14 · ARMOR CONDITION FIGURE: Six independent body segments glow amber when equipped and healthy, red at 30% condition or lower, and dark when destroyed or unequipped. The figure appears at left-middle while wearing the frame.

W20.13 · POWER ARMOR INSTRUMENTS: Helmet HUD rebuilt as amber analog gauges: HP and RADS left, a live direction compass centered, and fusion CORE, AP sprint stamina, and stacked AMMO on the right. No large boxes or upper visor overlays. Power-armor visuals, movement and sounds are unchanged.

W20.11 · SOUND TIMING: Footsteps use natural walk/sprint cadence, gunshots and impacts have longer decays, and power-armor hydraulics span the full entry animation.
