WEAPONTESTING.

W11.0 replaces the old fully-armored shell with a bare modular power frame: each armor section is hidden when its slot is empty, tap E near the frame to configure, and hold E to enter or exit. W10.0 introduces individual collectible T-45, T-60 and T-01 power-armor pieces, durability, repair, and Fusion Cores. W09.15 buffs NPC gun base damage (25 HP for standard carbines and assault rifles, with distinct values by weapon), restores each equipped armor piece's original percentage-based protection, and aligns each NPC's visible front and weapon with its actual aiming direction while keeping the old mannequin fully nonrendering. Armor is never weakened to compensate for a stronger gun; power armor still provides additional protection. W09.14 introduced six per-weapon NPC damage values, now retuned in W09.15. W09.13 makes the original NPC body the only rendered actor, fully hides legacy combat hitboxes, and fixes real firearm-specific RPM instead of shared role firing intervals. W09.12 removes overlapping body geometry, routes NPCs around solid cover, and adds six distinct NPC firearms with realistic semi-auto or automatic bursts. W09.11 gives enemies coordinated, fallible decisions: small fire teams split into angle guards, flankers and investigators; each reacts at its own speed and uses only confirmed sightings when you disappear. Test them by fighting, breaking contact and watching how they search. W09.9 improved combat AI: NPCs investigate the last place they actually saw you, sweep nearby corners, and stay alerted for roughly 45 seconds before returning to patrol. Scouts flank quickly, Raiders choose safer firing lanes, and Enforcers hold longer angles. They only crouch in real cover, and solid lab walls now block enemy sight, shots, and tracers. W09.8 adds inventory categories (Weapons, Armor, Clothing, Aid, Junk and Quest Items) and automatic ammo routing to weapon reserves. Ammo no longer consumes backpack slots and the HUD shows 999+ without capping the actual total. W09.7 removes all pre-equipped-looking headwear, goggles, chest gear, shoulder padding and knee pads from the ordinary survivor; the base outfit is now visibly just clothing. A field cap and canvas backpack are genuine looted inventory equipment, with EQUIP and UNEQUIP buttons. Six real armor slots appear only while equipped. Helmets automatically replace caps, and the player's worn appearance persists on reload. W09.6 replaces the imported hyper-realistic NPCs with original pixel-textured Scout, Raider, and Enforcer characters. Each role has a distinct silhouette, outfit, animated weapon, and visible equipped armor. Their existing tactical AI, body-part damage hitboxes, and loot are unchanged. W09.5 made regular armor plates bolder and added a second survivor preview near Station 8. That remote-preview body stays rendered when V toggles the local first-/third-person camera. It is a local visual test only, not online multiplayer. W09.4 connected ordinary armor to all six equipment slots, with distinct Scrap, Ranger, Militia and Citadel pixel-textured gear. The same survivor and equipped gear now appear during powered-armor entry and exit, replacing the imported realistic pilot. W09.3 introduced the first original playable survivor and V-key third-person inspection camera, while preserving the real FPS hitboxes and weapon raycasts. The powered chassis shell and its combat behavior remain unchanged. W08.4 added distinct NATO-style Holo A and Russian-style Holo B housings with their own reticles, plus an exact quantity selector and MAX option for dropping inventory stacks. W08.3 replaced the angular mannequin with a more compact armored exosuit: sculpted helmet and respirator, two-piece breastplate, rounded shoulder housings, articulated arms, hydraulic knees, and grounded armored boots. The cinematic entry remains, but the first-person eye height now follows standing and crouching normally while wearing power armor. Chest protection and all six optional armor plates are preserved. The six removable plates, armored cockpit and real damage protection still work. Get near the frame and tap E to configure parts, or hold E for 0.65 seconds to climb in or out. Previously W07.1 tightened and lightly faded the four lines of magnified scope reticles, and makes duplicate weapon pickups and inventory capacity easier to see. W07.0 brought an optics workbench with salvaged-material crafting, Holo A/B sights and actual 2×, 4× and 6× magnification. Station 7 has a physical bench (E), or press U to open it. Aiming uses the same camera line as the shot, with zero spread while standing still and aiming down sights. Previously, W06.7 improved long-range NPC combat: enemies now spot and shoot farther, Enforcers hold distant sightlines, and distant hits trigger return fire outside the arena. W06.6 removed the orange neck strip and balanced armor loot. NPCs still visibly wear mixed armor, but a defeat yields only a small random subset of the pieces they wore, never an entire Citadel suit. W06.5 added visible mixed armor on every NPC and matching drops. Reset the arena with J to see new outfits, from a single leg plate to a full suit. W06.4 fixed distant enemy updates, death cleanup and neck hitboxes. W06.0 introduced real RPG loot! Defeated enemies leave searchable world bags after their bodies disappear. Press E nearby to search, TAKE individual items or TAKE ALL, and press I to open your persistent 30-slot backpack. Equip looted guns and player armor, automatically collect ammo into each weapon reserve, use medkits and drop unwanted items. Rarity and cap values are shown on every item. Existing six-gun training and smarter enemies remain intact.
