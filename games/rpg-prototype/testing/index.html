GAMESHUB / RPG SYSTEMS LAB / W20.67.1

SYSTEMSTESTING.

W20.67 · NAVIGATION: Both HUDs now show explored locations and map-set custom waypoints with direction and distance. The weapon workbench uses only the left-hand owned-gun list; its redundant dropdown is gone. Existing merchant stock and equipment systems remain unchanged.

W20.62 · DEVELOPER ITEM GRANTS: Admins can open the Developer Panel to search implemented items, grant weapons, armor, ammo and materials, or add caps. Equip granted firearms through I / Backpack. Free crates, resource caches, and cap handouts have been retired from the test field. Normal loot, vendors and quests remain. Visit Station 7 to craft weapon mods with materials you own.

W20.62 · PLAYER INVENTORY Equipment and caps you already owned stay saved. New test saves start with no free guns, ammo reserves or field caches. Developer item grants are limited to signed-in admins; buyers still use actual vendor prices. The map, NPCs, power armor, weapon workbench and quest systems remain available.

W20.10 · REAR ENTRY & SUIT SILHOUETTE: Power armor has deploying rear stairs, an opening central spine, two-joint hip/knee IK, real handle-gripping arms and separate boot contacts. The camera stays outside until the hatch shuts. NPCs must face you before firing; tracers originate at their visible muzzle.

W20.18 · FAIR ENEMY RADAR: Red dots only reveal enemies after they have spotted you or you have engaged them. Contacts fade shortly after enemies lose sight; undiscovered patrols remain hidden. In power armor, tap E to search nearby enemy drops or crates, or hold E to exit.

W20.16 · COMBAT & POWER FRAME: Compact armor silhouette and clearance, deeper rear boarding, softer boot impacts, fusion cores lasting 25% longer, close-quarters enemy tracking, single-render-layer NPCs, concealed enemy tactics, and a bottom red-dot hostile compass.

W20.15 · COCKPIT CLEANUP: Removed the two obsolete teal camera-mounted LED strips that followed the player while wearing power armor. The amber gauges and armor-condition figure stay unchanged.

W20.14 · ARMOR CONDITION FIGURE: Six independent body segments glow amber when equipped and healthy, red at 30% condition or lower, and dark when destroyed or unequipped. The figure appears at left-middle while wearing the frame.

W20.13 · POWER ARMOR INSTRUMENTS: Helmet HUD rebuilt as amber analog gauges: HP and RADS left, a live direction compass centered, and fusion CORE, AP sprint stamina, and stacked AMMO on the right. No large boxes or upper visor overlays. Power-armor visuals, movement and sounds are unchanged.

W20.11 · SOUND TIMING: Footsteps use natural walk/sprint cadence, gunshots and impacts have longer decays, and power-armor hydraulics span the full entry animation.

Open the W20.55 field map with Tab or from your inventory. Enter power armor at Station 8 to test the analog HP, CORE, AP and AMMO dials. Turn to move the compass, sprint to drain AP, and take damage to move the HP needle.

The chassis starts as a bare exoskeleton, without pre-equipped plates. Obtain modules through normal gameplay or Developer Panel → Items & Caps, equip or strip them individually, or remove every plate to inspect the base frame. Each armor piece is a real salvageable item. Mix T-45, T-60 and T-01 pieces on the same chassis; a broken piece stays attached or in your backpack until repaired.
