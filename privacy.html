GAMESHUB Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how GAMESHUB handles information when you use the GAMESHUB website, Google Sign-In, and games such as REDLINE.

Information GAMESHUB uses

  • Google account identity: when you sign in with Google, GAMESHUB receives a Google ID token and uses the stable Google account identifier needed to recognize your account.
  • GAMESHUB profile information: the username you choose for use in games, leaderboards, and multiplayer.
  • Session information: session identifiers and timestamps used to keep you signed in and protect account sessions.
  • Standard technical request information may be processed by the hosting and infrastructure providers needed to operate the service.

Google user data

GAMESHUB uses Google Sign-In only for authentication and account identity. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or other Google service content. GAMESHUB does not store your Google password. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, keep usernames unique, maintain sessions, identify players in multiplayer, operate game features, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

Sharing

GAMESHUB does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that host or deliver GAMESHUB when necessary to run the service, and may be disclosed when required by law.

Retention and security

Account and profile information may be retained while an account is in use. Expired session records may be removed automatically. GAMESHUB uses server-side sessions and secure cookies where supported, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can stop using GAMESHUB at any time. For account or privacy questions, use the support contact shown on the Google consent/sign-in screen.

Changes

This policy may be updated when GAMESHUB features or data practices change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
