GAMESHUB is a browser-based game hub. Its current featured game, REDLINE, is a lightweight 3D street-racing game with multiplayer rooms, car selection, drifting, nitro, and race timing.

Why GAMESHUB uses Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used to create and recognize your GAMESHUB account. GAMESHUB uses your Google account identity to keep your account tied to one profile and lets you choose a GAMESHUB username for multiplayer. GAMESHUB does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, or other Google services.
